Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2025

This policy applies to the Stash Library Assistant GPT and the Stash Public API.

What data is processed

  • Bookmark URLs, titles, notes, and descriptions you submit
  • Search queries you enter
  • Collection and tag names
  • Your API key (used only to authenticate requests — never logged or stored by this GPT)

How data is used

Requests are forwarded to the Stash Public API (https://stzutoejnhzxzhjsjtsi.supabase.co/functions/v1/public-api) solely to retrieve or manage bookmarks stored in your Stash account. No data is used for training, advertising, or any purpose beyond fulfilling your request.

Data storage

This GPT does not independently store, log, or share your data. All bookmark data resides in your Supabase-hosted Stash account and is governed by Supabase's Privacy Policy.

Third parties

No data is shared with third parties. The only external service contacted is your own Stash API endpoint.

Your rights

You control all data in your Stash account. You can delete bookmarks, collections, or your entire account at any time from within the app.
