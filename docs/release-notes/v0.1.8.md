## Stash v0.1.8

> Running draft — fill in as milestones land, then cut `v0.1.8` from `main`.
> See `docs/development/milestones.md` → "0.1.8 cycle" for the plan.

A localization release: Stash now speaks Korean — UI and AI alike.

### ✨ Highlights

**Now in Korean (and translatable)**
- Full UI internationalization with a Korean (`ko`) locale; follows your device
  language with a manual override in Settings. English falls back for any
  untranslated string. (M11)

**AI in your language**
- AI summaries and tag suggestions come back in your language, and a
  rate-limited or unavailable model now degrades *visibly* instead of silently
  falling back to heuristics. (M12)
