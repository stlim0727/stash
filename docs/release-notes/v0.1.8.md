## Stash v0.1.8

> Running draft — fill in as milestones land, then cut `v0.1.8` from `main`.
> See `docs/development/milestones.md` → "0.1.8 cycle" for the plan.

A localization-and-retrieval release: Stash now speaks Korean, lets you search
and re-import your bookmarks, and edits tags fully offline.

### ✨ Highlights

**Now in Korean (and translatable)**
- Full UI internationalization with a Korean (`ko`) locale; follows your device
  language with a manual override in Settings. AI summaries/tags come back in
  your language too. (M11, M15)

**Find & restore your bookmarks**
- Instant client-side **search** across titles, URLs, notes, and tags — offline.
  (M12)
- **Import** a Stash JSON backup, idempotently (no duplicates) — completing the
  export round-trip from 0.1.7. (M13)

**Editing & AI**
- **Offline tag editing** — adding/removing tags is now optimistic and queued,
  like collection assignment. (M14)
- AI degrades gracefully and visibly when the model is rate-limited or down,
  instead of silently falling back. (M15)
