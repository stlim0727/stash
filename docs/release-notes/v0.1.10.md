## Stash v0.1.10

A maintenance release on the **0.1.x** line — share-capture and AI-suggestion fixes.

### 🐛 Fixes

- **Sharing returns you to the app you shared from.** In toast mode on Android,
  Stash now confirms with a floating toast and drops back to the background
  instead of leaving you parked in Stash. (#111)
- **No more empty AI suggestions on a fresh capture.** When a capture outran its
  metadata, the model saw a bare URL and returned nothing. Stash now sends the
  device's freshest metadata and defers the AI request until metadata settles —
  and already-affected bookmarks recover via "Refresh AI suggestions." (#112)
- **Deferred AI triggers survive a restart** and wait for an available auth
  session, so enrichment isn't lost if the app is closed right after capture.
- A shared link's durable record is now cleared **only after its save commits**,
  closing a small window where a capture could be dropped early (follow-up to
  the v0.1.9 durable-capture fix).
