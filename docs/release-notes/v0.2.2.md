## Stash v0.2.2

The debut of the **0.2** line: Stash now speaks Korean, brings AI into your
language, and gets a far smarter search. This is the first stable build since
v0.1.10 — it folds in everything from the 0.2.0 and 0.2.1 cycles (which were
never tagged) plus the 0.2.2 search epic.

### ✨ Highlights

**Now in Korean (and translatable)**
- Full UI internationalization with a Korean (`ko`) locale: Stash follows your
  device language, with a manual override in Settings, and English falls back
  for any untranslated string — never a blank or a visible key. (#131)
- A bilingual hero wordmark on the Inbox (Stash │ 스태시). (#131)
- Korean voice unified on the natural 합니다체 register throughout the app, with
  hand-written Korean copy rather than machine-calqued strings.

**AI in your language**
- AI summaries and tag suggestions now come back in *your* language, not the
  source page's — Korean in, Korean out — with locale-aware heuristic fallback
  tags so even the offline path stays in your language. (#132)
- A rate-limited or unavailable model now **degrades visibly**: instead of
  silently swapping in dummy heuristics, Stash tells you it's running in a
  reduced mode and how to recover, including a localized rate-limit note on the
  bookmark detail. (#132)
- AI suggestions are calmer and more reliable: they arrive ambiently rather than
  as a modal interruption, the folder-suggestion vocabulary is consistent across
  every screen, and recommendations now also show on the Review screen.
  (#141, #177, #210)

**Smarter search**
Search was rebuilt into the headline feature of this release. It now finds far
more, matches far more forgivingly, and guides you while you type.

- **Searches more of your bookmark.** Beyond title, URL, and notes, search now
  also matches your **tags**, **folder / collection names**, and the page's
  **site name** — so the words you actually remember a bookmark by all find it.
  (#198)
- **Forgiving, language-aware matching.** Queries are normalized so case,
  full-/half-width characters, accents, and punctuation no longer get in the way
  (NFKC, CJK- and Korean-aware). Type what you see on screen without spaces
  ("watchlater") and still match "Watch Later" — while symbol queries like
  `c++`, `c#`, and `.net` are preserved literally instead of being mangled into
  a useless broad term. (#198)
- **A suggestion chip shelf.** Tap into the search box and a single
  horizontally-scrolling row appears under the field with your **recent
  searches**, **top tags**, and **top folders** — your most likely next move,
  one tap away. Tap a recent to fill the query and keep editing; tap a tag or
  folder to jump straight to that facet. Long-press a recent to delete just that
  one. Recent searches are stored **on-device only** and never synced. (#198)
- **The shelf filters live as you type.** Start typing and the shelf narrows to
  the matching tags, folders, and recent searches, best-match-first, turning
  into an autocomplete rail rather than vanishing — and it updates on the same
  beat as the results, so the two never disagree. (#198)
- **Results show why they matched.** A result that matched on a tag promotes
  that tag as a small badge, and a site-name match shows a site chip, so it's
  always clear *why* a bookmark is in your results. (#198)
- **A zero-result recovery card** when nothing matches, instead of a blank
  screen. (#198)
- **Search scoped to a folder reads as such**: when a folder or tag facet is
  active, the placeholder becomes "Search in {folder}". (#209)
- **Clear search history** from Settings whenever you want a clean slate, in
  both English and Korean. (#209)

**Capture, organizing & the Inbox**
- **Trash replaces Archive.** Deleting now moves a bookmark to Trash with an
  **Undo toast**, the Settings layout was reorganized, and a soft version gate
  was added — no more confusing "Archive" ghost. (#173)
- **Save text and images, not just links.** No-link shared text (e.g. from
  KakaoTalk) is saved as a text note, and shared images are captured as local
  image bookmarks. (#151)
- **A friendlier, more discoverable Inbox.** A new onboarding empty state that
  teaches share-sheet capture, a tag-cloud Inbox view, an explicit ⋯ overflow
  button so card actions aren't long-press-only, a "last opened" sort with a
  cleaner sort menu, and an Inbox tag cloud that scopes to the active facet.
  (#127, #176)
- **Sign in inline from Settings**, right beside your identity, with a
  status-LED sync indicator. (#161)
- A redesigned app icon: a teal bookmark with a "+" save badge. (#136)

### 🐛 Fixes

- **Reliable AI folder suggestions across every surface** — durable and
  consistent whether they arrive on capture, refresh, or the Review screen,
  with one shared vocabulary. (#210)
- **No more stranded search shelf on Android**: dismissing the keyboard (Back
  button or list scroll) now closes the suggestion shelf instead of leaving it
  parked on screen. (#204)
- **Punctuation- or symbol-only queries are treated as "not searching"**, so the
  list and the header agree and you no longer see a misleading match label.
  (#207)
- **Returning to the tag cloud on Back** after drilling into a tag, with the
  Android Back handler registered only where it should be, and scroll offset
  reset when crossing the tag-cloud boundary. (#192, #174)
- **Sync correctness**: trashing a bookmark now propagates to the cloud, and
  tags are re-keyed correctly when an anonymous account's data carries over to a
  real one. (#196)
- Onboarding (not the tag cloud) now shows on a genuinely empty library, and the
  post-share confirmation toast has its own clear copy.
- Naver Map place previews are recovered via the server-rendered sibling page.
  (#145)

### Behind the scenes
- Backend hardening: the public REST API and `ai-enrich` edge function were
  audited and locked down (filter-injection hardening, `ai-enrich` fails closed,
  `app_config` row-level security scoped to known public keys), and API-key
  issuance is denied server-side by default. The external API-key UI was removed
  from the app while this posture is firmed up. (#197, #205, #211, #212)
