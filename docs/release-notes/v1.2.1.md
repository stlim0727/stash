## Stash v1.2.1

A small patch release on top of v1.2.0 fixing two Inbox search interaction bugs.

### 🐛 Fixes
- Fixed Inbox search-open focus feeling delayed behind a momentary scroll: opening search coincided with a `LayoutAnimation`-driven header/list reflow (native) and a header-height-driven list padding reflow (web), so the field appeared to focus late even though `.focus()` fired immediately (`#566`).
- Fixed the Inbox search close button not closing on desktop web: a `mousedown` on the toggle button blurred the still-focused search input before the click fired, and the blur's deferred empty-query auto-close ran first — flipping the button back to "search" and making the click reopen the field instead of closing it (`#567`).
