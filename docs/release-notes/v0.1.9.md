## Stash v0.1.9

A maintenance release on the **0.1.x** line — capture reliability.

### 🐛 Fixes

- **Shared links survive a background kill.** A URL shared to Stash is now
  written to durable storage the instant it's captured, so if Android suspends
  the app mid-share (common on a cold start) the bookmark is recovered and saved
  on the next launch instead of silently never appearing. (#115)
